# Code of conduct

This project is for people who want to build a good terminal client for Teams. To keep it a place where that is
pleasant to do, everyone taking part in issues, pull requests and discussions is expected to follow these rules.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Welcome newcomers and explain rather than dismiss.
- Keep discussion on the project, and give and take feedback gracefully.
- Respect other people's privacy. Do not post tokens, message content, names or other personal data from your own or
  anyone else's tenant. Masked recordings from `--record-shapes` are the safe way to show what a service sent.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks of any kind.
- Sexualised language or images, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Deliberately disrupting discussion, or anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and contributions that break these rules, and may restrict
participation by anyone who does. To report a problem, use the repository's private reporting channel (the **Security**
tab, **Report a vulnerability**, or a maintainer's contact details if the repository lists them) and say you are
reporting a conduct issue. Reports are handled privately.

This text is deliberately short. It is meant to be applied with common sense.
